# Organist Portfolio

Create a simple, elegant professional website for a female organist and organ teacher.

The main purpose of the website is to serve as her professional portfolio when applying for work and to allow people or organizations to contact and hire her as an organist, for example for weddings, ceremonies, church services, concerts and other occasions.

Keep the website simple, professional and personal. This is not a commercial booking platform. There should be no ticket sales, online booking system, login, payments or other complex functionality.

Create these main navigation sections/pages:

- Home
- About
- Experience
- Services
- Contact

Home:
A clean introduction with her name, a professional photo, a short description such as "Organist & Music Educator", and a simple call to action to contact her or learn more.

About:
A personal but professional biography, including her musical background, education and interests.

Experience:
Professional experience, education, notable performances, concerts and other relevant musical experience. Concerts are occasional, so do not create a dedicated concerts page or ticketing functionality.

Services:
Explain that she is available as an organist for weddings, ceremonies, church services, concerts, accompaniment and other suitable occasions. Also mention her work as a music/organ teacher.

Contact:
A very simple contact section with email and optional social media links. No complicated forms are necessary.

Visual style:
Use a light, warm and elegant design appropriate for a classical musician. Use a warm ivory/off-white background, dark charcoal text and subtle muted accent colors. Combine an elegant serif font for headings with a clean readable font for body text.

Keep the design minimalist and relatively static. Avoid excessive animations, gradients, glass effects, flashy transitions and overly modern startup-style design. It should feel classy, trustworthy, warm and professional.

Make the website fully responsive and make sure the mobile design is clean and simple.

For now, use placeholder text and placeholder images. Make sure to include couple of placeholder concerts she's had and some placeholder experiences.
